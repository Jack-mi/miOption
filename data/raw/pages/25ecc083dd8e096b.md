Our AI scans the options market daily and publishes call and put predictions with specific entry, target, and risk levels:

*   **Buy Target** — recommended entry price for the option contract
*   **1st & 2nd Sell Targets** — profit-taking levels where half the position is sold at each
*   **Stop Loss** — risk management level to limit downside

Active predictions are updated throughout market hours. Once a prediction concludes, it moves to the Closed Predictions archive with the simulated profit or loss percentage. Subscribers can view active signals in real time on the dashboard or fetch them programmatically via the [REST API](https://sharepredictions.com/api/docs/api-documentation/), while the full closed history and cumulative profit chart are available to everyone.

The P/L percentage reflects the premium price change based on the prediction outcome:

*   **Stop Loss only** — 100% of the position was closed at the stop-loss level
*   **1st Target + Stop Loss** — half sold at the 1st target profit, the other half hit stop loss; P/L is the average
*   **1st Target only** — half sold at the 1st target, remaining half expired; P/L is half the 1st target gain
*   **2nd Target** — half sold at the 1st target, half at the 2nd target; P/L is the average of both
*   **Expired** — position expired without hitting any target or stop loss

Hover over any P/L value in the closed predictions table to see the exact calculation for that specific prediction.
