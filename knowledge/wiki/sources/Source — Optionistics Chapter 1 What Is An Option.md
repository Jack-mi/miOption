# Source — Optionistics Chapter 1 What Is An Option

Defines calls and puts as rights (not obligations) to buy or sell; names the buyer the holder and the seller the writer. Mentions American vs European style. Buyer risk is limited to the premium; the writer of a call has theoretically unlimited risk. Does not replace OIC contract wording.
