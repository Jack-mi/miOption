# Bid-ask spread and liquidity cost

The bid–ask spread is the gap between the immediate buy price (ask) and the immediate sell price (bid). Wikipedia treats its size as a measure of market liquidity and of transaction cost, including for options.

## Terms

- bid
- ask
- quoted spread
- effective spread
- liquidity

## Content

A liquidity demander (market order) pays the spread; a liquidity supplier (limit order) earns it. On a standardized exchange, brokerage fees and the bid–ask spread make up almost all of the transaction cost. Quoted spread is ask minus bid (often as a percent of the midpoint). Effective spread uses actual trade prices and is typically smaller when dealers improve on the quote.

This page is general market-structure text. It mentions options as one of the instruments that quote a spread; it does not publish option-specific typical spread widths.
