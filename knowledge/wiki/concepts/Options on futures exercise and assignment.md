# Options on futures exercise and assignment

An option on a futures contract gives the right to go long or short that **futures** contract, not to take delivery of the physical commodity on the option’s expiration. Exercise and assignment create a futures position at the strike.

## Terms

- option on futures
- exercise
- assignment
- American-style
- European-style
- premium

## Content

Buyers hold the right; only they decide to exercise. Sellers are assigned at random by the clearing house via the brokerage firm. A long call exercise becomes a long futures; a long put exercise becomes a short futures. American-style can be exercised any trading day through expiration; European-style only on expiration. Buyers pay the full premium (no margin on purchase) and can lose at most that premium. Sellers post margin; profit is limited to premium received and loss is described as unlimited.
