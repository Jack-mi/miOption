# Options margin and account approval

Long options are paid in full; you cannot buy them on stock-style margin. Writing uncovered options typically requires a margin account, broker approval, and extra collateral.

## Terms

- margin account
- margin call
- premium margin
- uncovered writer
- approval level

## Content

OIC: brokerage firms approve investors for a specific options level (often four or five). More experience and liquid assets generally mean a higher level. Writing uncovered calls is an example of a transaction that may have to sit in a margin account. The minimum margin for uncovered writers is 20% of the underlying security minus the out-of-the-money amount, but never less than 10% of the security value. If the account falls below maintenance, the firm can make a margin call and, if unpaid, liquidate assets.

Wikipedia: margin is collateral posted against credit risk, including from a derivative contract. Option sellers deposit **premium margin** equal to the cost to buy the option back, plus additional margin for a worst-case next-day move. U.S. futures exchanges often use SPAN for options and futures. Covered calls generally have less strict requirements than naked call writing.
